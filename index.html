How to Play

The idea

You start with a Town Hall and three villagers. Gather food, wood, stone and gold, build houses and workshops, raise an army, and advance through the Dawn, Hearth, Forge and Empire ages. Each age unlocks stronger soldiers, new buildings and technologies. Win by destroying every rival's buildings and villagers, or by finishing a Monument and holding it for five minutes.

Economy

Right-click a tree, bush or deposit with villagers selected to gather. They carry loads back to the nearest drop-off: the Town Hall, or a Granary, Lumber Camp or Mining Camp built next to the resource. Berries run out, so build Farms; one villager works each farm forever. Every five people need a House.

Ages

Select the Town Hall and choose Advance. Each age needs two different buildings from the age before it and a stock of food and gold. Buildings change their look as you climb.

Fighting

Spearmen beat horses, horses beat archers, archers beat spearmen. Swordsmen and knights are the heavy hitters of the Forge Age; catapults flatten buildings in the Empire Age. Idle soldiers fight back on their own; use Attack move to advance while engaging anything met on the way. Watchtowers and Keeps shoot at anything hostile within range. The Blacksmith upgrades weapons and armour; the Hall of Scholars improves the economy.

Controls

SelectLeft click, or drag a box. Shift adds. Double-click selects all of a type on screen.
CommandRight click: move, gather, build, repair or attack depending on the target. Right-click with a building selected sets its rally point.
CameraArrow keys, middle-drag or space-drag, mouse wheel to zoom. Click the minimap to jump.
HotkeysThe letter on each command button (Q W E R T Y / A S D F G / Z X C V B). H Town Hall · . next idle villager · Enter last event · Ctrl+1–9 save a group, 1–9 recall · Delete remove selected · Esc cancel / menu.
TouchTap to select, tap the ground or a target to command, drag to pan, pinch to zoom.
